# SEO Blog Post Template

Use this structure for every new article.

## Metadata

- Slug: short, keyword-focused, lowercase.
- Title: 45-60 characters when possible.
- Description: 140-160 characters with audience, benefit, and keyword.
- Target keyword: one primary long-tail keyword.
- Secondary keywords: three to six related phrases.

## Article Structure

```text
H1: Primary keyword plus clear benefit

Intro:
- Name the audience.
- Name the problem.
- Promise a practical outcome.

H2: Step or concept 1
- Explain in plain Indian English.
- Add one fresher or internship example.

H2: Step or concept 2
- Include resume wording examples.
- Link internally to a related Apply guide.

H2: Common mistakes
- Keep this scannable.
- Use bullets if there are more than three mistakes.

H2: How Apply helps
- Connect the topic to upload, ATS matching, tailoring, PDF export, or interview prep.

Conclusion:
- One clear next action.
```

## Internal Links

Add at least two internal links:

- One link to another blog guide.
- One link to `/dashboard/generate` or the home page.

## Quality Rules

- Do not publish fake ratings, fake student counts, or fake press mentions.
- Avoid keyword stuffing.
- Use terms Indian students actually search for: fresher, internship, campus placement, engineering student, IT companies, TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Mumbai, Delhi NCR.
- Keep advice truthful and interview-safe.
