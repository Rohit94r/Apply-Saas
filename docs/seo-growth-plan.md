# Apply SEO Growth Plan

Goal: make `apply.neexmeet.com` easier for Google to understand and give it useful public content for Indian students and freshers.

## Week 1

- Deploy the updated metadata, structured data, sitemap, robots, FAQ, and blog pages.
- Verify Google Search Console.
- Submit `https://apply.neexmeet.com/sitemap.xml`.
- Inspect the home page and the first blog post URL.

## Week 2

- Add two more practical posts from real student pain points.
- Add screenshots or short product visuals to blog posts where useful.
- Link blog posts to each other and to the resume generator.
- Check Search Console indexing coverage.

## Week 3

- Share guides in student communities, placement prep groups, LinkedIn, and college tech clubs.
- Ask college placement cells or student clubs to link to the free resume builder if they find it useful.
- Improve posts based on Search Console queries with impressions but low CTR.

## Monthly Checks

- Organic clicks and impressions.
- Top queries and average position.
- Landing page conversion rate to resume upload.
- Blog post CTR.
- Core Web Vitals for mobile.

## Important

No SEO work can guarantee rank 1. The strongest path is technical cleanliness, useful public content, real backlinks, fast pages, and product trust signals that are true.
